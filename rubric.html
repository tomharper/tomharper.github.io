Scoring you can stand behind

A coding widget is a commodity. The hard, valuable part is an evaluation that's fair, defensible, and holds up to scrutiny. That's what Rubric is built around.

📋

Rubric-anchored

Every answer is scored against explicit criteria with worked anchors — not a vibe. Two candidates are actually comparable.

🔍

Evidence-linked

No score without proof. Each rating cites the exact moments in the transcript it's based on, so any decision is inspectable and contestable.

⚖️

Bias-tested

The judge is continuously checked for bias — the same answer, varied only by an irrelevant signal, must score the same. Fails the check, it doesn't get used.

🎚️

Adaptive

Questions adjust to the candidate — deepen where they're strong, ease where they're not — to find real signal fast, without repeating.

🙋

Human in the loop

The AI produces evidence; a person makes the call. Clear cases fast-track; the close ones route to a human, who can override with a logged reason.

🗂️

Auditable

Every session is an append-only record tied to a rubric version. Replay it, contest it, or hand it to an auditor. Built for regulated hiring.

The AI's output is evidence for a decision — not the decision. A person always makes the call, and every score can be traced back to what the candidate actually said.

How it works

Configure once; every candidate runs the same fair, adaptive gauntlet.

Configure the interview

Define the rounds, the rubric dimensions and weights, and where the pass/review/reject lines sit. Scores are versioned to this config.

Calibrate the judge

Before it's trusted, the evaluator is checked against ground truth and tested for bias. A judge that doesn't pass doesn't score real candidates.

Candidate interviews — adaptively

An AI interviewer asks, follows up, and adjusts difficulty to the candidate. Voice or text. Every turn is captured on one timeline.

Evidence-linked scoring

Each rubric dimension is scored with quoted evidence and a confidence — and it abstains rather than guess when the signal isn't there.

A human decides

Clear results fast-track; the ambiguous middle routes to a person, who confirms or overrides with a recorded reason. Fully auditable.
