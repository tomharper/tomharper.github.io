Work-Out Sim — Monte Carlo idea generator

You bring the problem. A population of grounded noses argues it out, thousands of times, with the dice shaken. The product is a distribution, not a meeting.

Rare goldlow freq × high agreement — ideas only a specific lens finds, but everyone nods when they do. The quadrant a single Work-Out session can't see.
Obvious winshigh freq × high agreement — you probably already knew. Good sanity check that the room isn't broken.
Noiselow freq × low agreement — prune. Signal that some noses are chasing hobbyhorses.
Contested evergreenshigh freq × low agreement — the stuff every retro re-litigates. Worth naming, rarely worth solving in this session.
